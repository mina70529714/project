About The Project عن المشروع

A graduation project with real-world impact مشروع تخرج بأثر حقيقي

CareBridge is a comprehensive rehabilitation center management system designed to bridge the gap between healthcare providers and patients' families through smart digital solutions.

The system unifies patient management, session scheduling, progress tracking, and family communication into one cohesive platform across web, mobile, and desktop environments.

"Empowering rehabilitation centers with technology to deliver better patient outcomes."
